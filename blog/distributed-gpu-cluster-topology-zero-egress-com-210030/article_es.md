## 1. Cómo Eliminar Cuellos de Botella Invisibles en Memoria

Los promedios ocultan los picos del percentil noventa y nueve. Utilizar grupos de memoria preasignados y topologías deterministas garantiza sesenta cuadros por segundo estables y reduce drásticamente el gasto innecesario en la nube.

## Studio Dialogue Highlights

- **Atlas:** The ocean floor is currently screaming with 400 gigabits of wasted latency because we insisted on centralizing compute. Why pay a tax for light to travel?
- **Kaan:** Because the cloud providers convinced the board that 'owning' a data center is like owning a pet—expensive, messy, and prone to dying on weekends.
- **Lara:** But if we move to distributed GPU clusters, aren't we just trading one kind of mess for another? I mean, how do you even orchestrate that chaos?
- **Atlas:** It’s not chaos if you treat the network as the backplane. We stop treating nodes as isolated silos and start treating the geography as a performance variable.
- **Kaan:** Zero-egress sounds like a marketing slogan cooked up by someone who's never had to pay an AWS bill after a heavy data migration month.
- **Atlas:** It’s not a slogan, Kaan. It’s a design paradigm. When compute happens at the edge where the data resides, you move bits, not petabytes. You kill the transit cost.
